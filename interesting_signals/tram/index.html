Franklin Street Signal Box

Located at the intersection of Swanston and Franklin Streets, this signal box was once used as the main control point for the tram terminus here, before the terminus was moved to Melbourne University.
The track that used to be the terminus has since been removed, as of 2025.
There is a single aspect 'ats Sydney' red light at the top left of the signal box, although I'm not too sure what it's purpose served.
Close up images of the aforementioned light.

Single aspect white lights

Around the Melbourne CBD (and occasionally outside of it), there are many single aspect white lights dotted around, at tram stops.
They are programmed to supposedly light up to advise a tram to depart the tram stop, so that it will get tram priority at an upcoming intersection; however so far I've never seen this happen.
Shows how much VicRoads cares about tram priority!...

Southbank Tram Depot

An assortment of halogen/incandescent signals.

Right: 2 aspect 'ats Durasig' variant tram light.
This arrangement, with a yellow light at top and white at bottom is common around tram depots, although I have no idea what it means.
Left: 2 aspect red 'T' lights - These are at the western entry of the depot, facing exiting trams. This light is red by default, and turns off when a button on this pole is pressed. It also triggers a red 'T' light at the Montague Street tram stop for eastbound trams, so that trams going onto the mainline while shunting in the depot do not conflict with trams on the mainline.

Right: 2 aspect red arrows, also for trams - At the eastern depot entrance. This light is also red by default, and turns off when eastbound trams get a red signal, allowing a tram to enter/shunt into the depot without conflict.
Montague St tram stop:
Left: The aforementioned 'T' light that stops eastbound trams from conflicting with shunting trams at the western depot entry point.
Right: Single aspect Aldridge Group 12" light - This light faces the opposite way toward the western depot entry, and is lit when the 'T' light is red as confirmation of it working.

Brunswick Tram Depot

An assortment of mostly LED signals - Yes, the photos aren't the best; I will attempt to get clearer photos of these lights in the future.

Left: The main light that all trams see when approaching the depot - A single aspect 'A' light; a 2 aspect incandescent/halogen light, and a 4 aspect LED light.
I presume the 'A' light is for showing if the lights are in automatic mode. The 2 aspect signal has the bottom light flashing by default, and I have no idea what it means.

The 4 aspect LED light is quite interesting; the module arrangement is as such (From the top):
1: White dash (Similar to points indicators)
2: Red 'T' light
3: Yellow 'T' light
4: 3 digit 7-segment display - Maybe for identifying which road a tram will head into?

Right: A similar setup as the light before, but without the 7-seg. display and the incandescent light; for exiting the tram depot. Interesting, the 3 aspect here is actually in an AWA Traffic Systems housing, which will be covered in future.
