Aldridge Traffic Group 'CLS' LED signals

The term 'CLS' stands for 'central light source', meaning there is a single light source within the LED light.

First Generation - Manufactured Mid 2010's

These lights have a more even light output compared to a regular Aldridge LED, and the arrows for this have a 'squished' shape to them. These lights only came in ball, arrow, and pedestrian signal variants.

A different design of pedestrian light:

At the pedestrian crossing at Bosisto St on Bridge Rd, there are 2 pedestrian lights that have a unique female person design, and they were installed as part of a commemoration of Mary Rogers, Victoria's first female councillor.

Interestingly, in the linked article, these lights were supposed to be installed for only 12 months, however has remained since 2016.


Alternative arrow design for First Generation CLS:

At the intersection of Bridge Rd/Church St, there are two traffic signals with an alternative arrow design, similar to the Second Generation of CLS arrows.
This is easily seen by the arrow shape being 'painted' onto the lens (for lack of a better term).


Second Generation - Manufactured Late 2010's - present

These lights, similar to the First Generation of Aldridge CLS LED light, have a even light output, however the lens design has been changed to a 'diamond' pattern.
These lights do look similar to the First Generation of Tyco LED light, however are slighty different in lens design and light output angles.
A thing to note - White CLS lights do not have the same lens design as those of other colours, instead having a lens design more akin to the ATS LED pedestrian light lens design.
