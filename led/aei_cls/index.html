Aldridge Electrical Industries CLS LED signals

Manufactured: Mid-2020's - current

The housing design is identical to the AEI LED signal housing, however the LED modules & lenses have changed significantly.
The roundel signals have had their lenses changed to ones that look like those from 90's Aldridge incandescent signals. The pedestrian and bike lights have had their lenses replaced with clear lenses, akin to those on the First Generation roundel signals.
