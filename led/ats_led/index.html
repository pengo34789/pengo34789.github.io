Aldridge Traffic Systems LED signals

Manufactured: 2000's

A common version of Aldridge LED light, made in 8" & 12" variations, although 12" lights are not used in Victoria. These have ATS branding on the front and back.

Nearly all installations of these lights of this version use cutaway/scoop visors exclusively.

High density revision

Manufactured in the late 2000's.

These lights have a very high density of LEDs compared to other lights, identical to that of the Aldridge Clear LEDs (revision 2).

'Hexagonal' LED arrangement revision

Manufactured mid-late 2000's.

I call these 'hexagonal' as in the centre of the red and green roundel modules, there is a hexagon shape.
The yellow roundel seems to be the same design as some other ATS LEDs.
