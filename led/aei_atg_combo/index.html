Aldridge Traffic Group LED signal - Other revision

Manufactured: Early-mid 2010's

An unique version of an ATS signal - The back housing is very similar to an Aldridge Electrical Industries light (with vertical ridges,) however it has Aldridge Traffic Systems branding with the Aldridge Traffic Group 'rocket' logo - in essence, an amalgamation of 3 branding revisions. The front has regular Aldridge Traffic Group branding.

The only locations that this type of signal has been found at is at the Anakie Rd railway crossing, in Bell Park, Geelong, VIC; and the Beckett St pedestrian railway crossing, in Jacana, VIC.
These are also the only railway crossings in Victoria to have pedestrian signals in the first place.
