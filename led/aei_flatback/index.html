Aldridge Electrical Industries LED signals

Manufactured: Mid-late 2010's

An uncommon version of 8" Aldridge LED light (Unsure if 12" lights exist.) These have a little 'E' on the front for branding, and the front door is held on with plastic clasps. The back has AEI branding on it, and is relatively smooth and rounded.

The housing door design is copied from the Bob Panich light design, a company that Aldridge bought out in 2012. I have only been able to find arrow, ball, and pedestrian signal variants.

The LED array of the ball and arrow modules are different to those of other Aldridge LED modules, and the pedestrian LED module lens is unique.
