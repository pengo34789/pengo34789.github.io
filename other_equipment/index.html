Aldridge Electrical Industries ATU

First revision made during 2000's (possibly 1990's also); second revision made during 2010's and 2020's.
First revision AEI ATU's have 'ridges' similar to AEI incandescent lights; the second revision has ommitted them. First revision ATU's are very common around Melbourne.
