Aldridge Traffic Systems 8" & 12" incandescent/halogen signal

Manufactured: Mid-1990's to late-2000's

Early revision

Manufactured in the mid-late 1990's; the less common revision.

8" lights of this revision does not have any branding on the housing, only branding on the front with blocky text, like the 'Aldridge Electrical' revision.
12" lights have retained all branding from the 'Aldridge Electrical' light design, albeit with branding changed to 'Aldridge Traffic Systems'.

Later revision

Manufactured throughout the 2000's, and are very common.

The logo on the front has been changed to a more rounded font. 'Aldridge Traffic Systems' branding has been added onto the back, on the housing below where the wires come out (Single aspect signals hence do not have branding on the back.)

I have not been able to find any examples of a 12" variant - Aldridge may have simply reused the earlier revision design.
