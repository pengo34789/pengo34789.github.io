Aldridge Traffic Systems 8" "Flatback" incandescent

Manufactured: 1980's

One of the 3 oldest styles of ATS incandescent lights. These have a distinct flat profile, and are made from aluminium. The lenses have the 'ATS' logo embossed in the centre.
The housings are likely derived from the G+W Eagle Signal 8" housing design, albeit without the hinged doors mounted externally.

Looking from the front, these look identical to the other 3 ATS light designs from this era.
These lenses are also what AWA used on their later lights.

12" variants of this light did exist, although they have only been documented to have been used formerly on the Victorian Railway network as route indicators.
