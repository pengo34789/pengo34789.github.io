Aldridge Traffic Systems 8" "ats Sydney" incandescent signal

Manufactured: 1980's

One of the 3 oldest styles of ats incandescent lights. These are made from plastic, and were later copied by AWA for their lineup of incandescent lights.
The lenses have the 'ats' logo embossed in the centre.

Looking from the front, these look identical to the other 3 ATS light designs from this era.
