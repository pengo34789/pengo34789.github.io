Aldridge Group 12" Incandescent signal

Manufactured: 1980's

Prototype Version:

There is a prototype version of this light (at least I'd assume it's a prototype), likely developed after Aldridge bought out the Eagle Signal Company of Australia in 1984.
The light has similar hinges as an Eagle Signal 12" light, however the housing is plastic. The back of the housing has the Safetran Systems Corp. logo on it.
The doors have a sticker with Aldridge Group/Aldridge Automatic Systems branding, and also has 'Lexalite' moulded into the door.

Photos of the prototype signal:
Lennox St/Highett St, Richmond, VIC. The only example of this light in existence.

Non-Prototype signals:

Below are examples of non-prototype lights - These are the most common type of 12" Aldridge light in Victoria.

Like the Aldridge Group 8" variant, these lights use 240vac bulbs, with no transformer inside.
